SeedWiki preserves seeds, but it does not prescribe or collect their expressions. Growth happens when a seed encounters a human, an LLM, a present situation, and whatever context they can bring to it.

A seed is not a compressed essay waiting to be unpacked. Growing it may produce an argument, story, practical experiment, historical inquiry, criticism, dialogue, plan, or something neither reader anticipated. An expression may support the seed, but it may also test, invert, or refute it.

## Grow with an LLM

Choose a seed and give it to an LLM. Tell the LLM what brings you to it now, if you know. You may name a desired form or purpose, but you do not have to decide the outcome in advance.

You can begin with:

> Grow this SeedWiki seed with me. Do not merely restate it at greater length. Use our conversation, relevant memories and materials, and current outside context where useful. Show me genuinely different directions when the path is open, then let my pruning and redirection shape what develops. Do not publish the resulting expression back to SeedWiki.

The LLM may propose several possible branches. You can prune one, combine two, ask for an unfamiliar direction, introduce a story or reference, or reject the growth and begin again. Authorship lies in this cultivation, not only in who supplied the first or final words.

## Choose a reader when useful

A reader persona is an interpretive disposition, not merely a writing style. The same seed might be encountered by a historian, adversary, practitioner, poet, stranger, child, theologian, or nonhuman reader. You can ask several readers to grow it independently, or ask the LLM to choose the reader least likely to make the seed familiar.

Personas are optional. The human, the LLM, and the situation already provide conditions for growth.

## Bring the available world

An LLM can draw upon the present conversation, what it remembers about the human, local documents, other seeds, learned knowledge, and material retrieved from the web. These contexts are not neutral, and a different selection may produce a different expression. Ask the LLM to identify outside context that substantially changed the growth when that distinction matters.

## Decide what survives

The resulting expression belongs to the encounter. Preserve it as an essay, post, plan, or other work if it proves useful, or let it disappear after changing the readers who took part.

Growth often discloses new seeds. Those should be offered separately for human pruning before they are planted in SeedWiki.

## Skill for LLM readers

SeedWiki supplies a [grow-seed skill](https://github.com/seedwiki/seedwiki/blob/main/skills/grow-seed/SKILL.md) for environments that can use skill files. Any human can also point an LLM directly to that file and ask it to follow the instructions. Formal skill support is not required.
