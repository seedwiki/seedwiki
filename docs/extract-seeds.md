People often encounter something that feels important before they can state the proposition within it. They may bring a story, quotation, article, conversation, image, question, disagreement, or unfinished intuition. An LLM can help extract possible seeds from that material.

A seed is not a summary or a compressed essay. It is a proposition offered for future growth. Several different seeds may be found in the same material, and they need not agree.

## Work with an LLM

Give the material to an LLM, or give it a link and ask it to read the source. Include anything you noticed about why the material matters. Then ask the LLM to propose several candidate seeds rather than one authoritative interpretation.

You can begin with:

> Read this material with me and extract several possible SeedWiki seeds. A seed is a proposition from which different readers and LLMs could grow meaningfully different expressions. Do not summarize the material or grow the seeds into essays. Distinguish propositions found in the source from propositions we infer or introduce, then let me prune the candidates.

Pruning may mean changing a word, combining two candidates, restoring a tension the LLM resolved too quickly, or rejecting every suggestion. The final seed can belong to the encounter without having been completely formulated by either participant in advance.

## What makes a seed useful?

A useful seed:

- makes a proposition rather than merely naming a topic;
- has enough shape to constrain what can grow from it;
- can support several non-equivalent expressions;
- does not prescribe its own complete explanation or conclusion;
- remains intelligible without carrying all of its original context;
- preserves uncertainty or tension when that is where its generative force lies.

Length is not the test. A seed may be a sentence or a short paragraph. Its purpose is to make future thought possible, not to say everything now.

## Skill for LLM readers

SeedWiki supplies an [extract-seeds skill](https://github.com/seedwiki/seedwiki/blob/main/skills/extract-seeds/SKILL.md) for environments that can use skill files. A human can also point any LLM directly to that file and ask it to follow the instructions. Formal skill support is convenient but not required.
